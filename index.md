# The Great Matter

-----

## Motivation

> "There is nothing here that has not been said before, and I have no skill in the art of composition. Therefore I write this only to acquaint it to my mind." — [Śāntideva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9A%C4%81ntideva)

-----

## The Field

Alexander crossed continents, leaving nations buried in hoofprints. He arrived at a field where sages stood, unmoved by the dust of his army.

A sage stamped the ground.

> "King Alexander, everyone can possess only so much of the earth's surface as this we are standing on. You are human like the rest of us, except you are always busy and up to no good, traveling so far from your home, a nuisance to yourself and to others. Ah well! You will soon be dead, and then you will own just as much of this earth as needed to bury you." — [Arrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrian)

Alexander stood in the field. His empire shrank to the size of his sandals.

-----

### Practice

Pause. Notice the ground under your feet.

Ask: *Is this not enough?*

-----

### Dialogue

Student: "What was the sage's teaching?"
Sage: "The hoofprints in the dust."
Student: "And Alexander's realization?"
Sage: "The dust settling again."

-----

## Three Poisons: Why Is This Not Enough?

Alexander conquered and still thirsted. Why?

**Greed** ([Lobha / Rāga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raga_%28Buddhism%29)) — the conviction that fulfillment is always one conquest away. A bottomless cup.

**Hatred** ([Dosa / Dveṣa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvesha_%28Buddhism%29)) — the reflex to destroy what resists us. Alexander razed Persepolis. The fire went out. The hatred did not.

**Delusion** ([Moha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moha_%28Buddhism%29)) — the root. The belief in a separate self that must be fed and defended. Alexander saw "Alexander" everywhere.

-----

### Practice

Pause. Reflect on today.

Ask: *Why is this not enough?*

-----

### Dialogue

Student: "Which poison is worst?"
Sage: "The one you don't notice."
Student: "How do I notice it?"
Sage: "Who is asking?"

-----

## Three Marks: What Is This?

But what is this? What did Alexander glimpse?

**Impermanence** ([Anicca / Anitya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impermanence)) — Everything that arises, passes. Alexander's empire. Alexander's body. Clinging to any of it is gripping sand.

**Non-Self** ([Anattā / Anātman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatta)) — No fixed "Alexander" inside Alexander. Sensations, feelings, thoughts — a stream mistaken for a mountain.

**Dissatisfaction** ([Dukkha / Duḥkha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du%E1%B8%A5kha)) — The friction between what is and what we demand. Alexander pressed his will upon the world. The world did not comply.

-----

### Practice

Sit. Watch things arise. Watch things vanish.

Ask: *What is this?*

-----

### Dialogue

Student: "If nothing is self, who suffers?"
Sage: "The question suffers."

-----

## Three Trainings: What To Do With This?

The poisons are habitual. Training is required.

**Wisdom** ([Paññā / Prajñā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praj%C3%B1%C4%81_%28Buddhism%29)) — Nothing arises alone. Alexander's ambition required his father's death, his tutor's maps, his soldiers' obedience, grain from conquered fields. Pull one thread and the conqueror unravels.

**Virtue** ([Sīla / Śīla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_ethics)) — Do no harm. Not as commandment, but as consequence of seeing clearly. When the illusion of separation thins, harming another is harming yourself.

**Concentration** ([Samādhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam%C4%81dhi_%28Buddhism%29)) — Gather the mind and rest. The sage stood in the field, undisturbed. Alexander and the army arose and fell of their own accord.

-----

### Practice

Sit. For five minutes, just breathe. When you wander, return.

Ask: *What should I do with this?*

-----

### Dialogue

Student: "Where do I begin?"
Sage: "You are already in the field."
Student: "I don't see any sages."
Sage: "Stop looking at the army."

-----

## Coda

Alexander died at thirty-two in Babylon. The field is still here.
